Boris Prokhorov

I am a PhD student in the EPFL Theory Group, advised by Michael Kapralov.

My research focuses on efficient, practical algorithms for high-dimensional problems. Recently, I have been studying nearly optimal algorithms for approximating attention in a beyond-worst-case setting.
